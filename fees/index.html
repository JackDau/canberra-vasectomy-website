Deposit

A $200 deposit is paid at the time of booking and is subtracted from the procedure fee. The deposit is refundable if 24 hours notice is given before the operation.

Medicare Rebates

Medicare rebates are processed on the day and typically appear in your bank account within 1-2 business days. No private health insurance is required.
