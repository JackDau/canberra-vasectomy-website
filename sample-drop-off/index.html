SAMPLE DROP-OFF LOCATIONS

After your vasectomy you will need to provide a semen sample for testing (a post-vasectomy semen analysis). The test is done 3 months after your procedure. Drop your sample at whichever location below is most convenient — you can use any 4Cyte Pathology collection centre (find your nearest one at 4cyte.com.au/locations) or any of the sites listed.

Patients from regional NSW: the collector may be unfamiliar with handling post-vasectomy samples and may query it when you drop it off. If this happens, show them the note below — you can also download it to take with you.

Please phone ahead where noted. Some centres only accept semen samples at certain times or by appointment, so it is worth calling first to confirm.

If the collection centre queries your sample

Some collectors are not familiar with post-vasectomy samples. If you are asked, show them this note — you can also download it as a PDF to take with you.

This is a post-vasectomy sperm test.

Please note: the timeframe for laboratory review is not clinically significant in this case. There is no urgency for processing upon receipt at the lab.

Kindly provide the specimen to your usual courier service for routine collection and delivery.

Thank you for your assistance.

Yours faithfully,
Dr John Deery
